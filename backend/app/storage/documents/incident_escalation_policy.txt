ENTERPRISE INCIDENT ESCALATION POLICY
Version: 5.2
Department: IT Service Management
Last Updated: March 2026

============================================================
1. OVERVIEW
============================================================

This document defines the enterprise incident escalation
framework for IT support operations.

The purpose of escalation management is to:
- Minimize downtime
- Ensure rapid resolution
- Maintain SLA compliance
- Coordinate cross-functional response

============================================================
2. INCIDENT SEVERITY LEVELS
============================================================

LOW:
- Minor inconvenience
- Single-user issue
- No business disruption

MEDIUM:
- Department-level issue
- Limited business impact
- Workaround available

HIGH:
- Multiple departments affected
- Significant productivity impact
- No reliable workaround

CRITICAL:
- Enterprise-wide outage
- Security breach
- Core infrastructure failure

============================================================
3. ESCALATION TIERS
============================================================

============================================================
TIER 1 SUPPORT
============================================================

Responsibilities:
- Ticket intake
- Basic troubleshooting
- Password resets
- User guidance

Escalation Criteria:
- Issue unresolved after standard troubleshooting
- Administrative privileges required
- Infrastructure involvement required

============================================================
TIER 2 SUPPORT
============================================================

Responsibilities:
- Advanced diagnostics
- Application troubleshooting
- Network analysis
- Hardware replacement coordination

Escalation Criteria:
- Core infrastructure impacted
- Multiple systems affected
- Security involvement required

============================================================
TIER 3 SUPPORT
============================================================

Responsibilities:
- Infrastructure recovery
- Root cause analysis
- Vendor coordination
- Enterprise remediation

============================================================
4. INCIDENT RESPONSE WORKFLOW
============================================================

STEP 1 — INCIDENT DETECTION

Incident sources:
- User tickets
- Monitoring systems
- Security alerts
- Automated workflows

------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 2 — INCIDENT CLASSIFICATION

Assign:
- Severity
- Priority
- Category
- Responsible department

------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 3 — INITIAL RESPONSE

Perform:
- User communication
- Service impact assessment
- Temporary mitigation if possible

------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 4 — ESCALATION

Escalate according to:
- Severity level
- SLA deadlines
- Technical complexity

------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 5 — RESOLUTION

After resolution:
- Verify service restoration
- Notify stakeholders
- Update documentation

============================================================
5. COMMUNICATION REQUIREMENTS
============================================================

For HIGH and CRITICAL incidents:
- Notify management immediately
- Send periodic status updates
- Maintain incident bridge communication

============================================================
6. SLA GUIDELINES
============================================================

LOW:
- Response: 8 business hours
- Resolution: 3 business days

MEDIUM:
- Response: 4 business hours
- Resolution: 1 business day

HIGH:
- Response: 1 hour
- Resolution: 4 hours

CRITICAL:
- Immediate response required
- Continuous remediation until resolved

============================================================
7. SECURITY INCIDENT HANDLING
============================================================

Security incidents require:
- Immediate escalation
- Incident isolation
- Security Operations involvement
- Audit log preservation

============================================================
8. POST-INCIDENT REVIEW
============================================================

Major incidents require:
- Root cause analysis
- Timeline reconstruction
- Preventive action planning
- Documentation updates
